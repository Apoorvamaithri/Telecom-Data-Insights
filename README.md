# Atliqo Telecom Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built for AtliQ Technologies to analyze telecom revenue, user activity, and market share — with a specific focus on comparing performance **Before 5G** vs. **After 5G** rollout.

![Dashboard Preview](atliqo_telecom_dashboard.png)

## Overview

This report helps telecom business stakeholders answer:
- How has 5G rollout impacted revenue and active users over time?
- Which plans generate the most revenue?
- How does AtliQ's market share compare to competitors?
- Which cities contribute the most (and least) to revenue, and how does that break down by 5G status?

## Key Metrics (KPI Cards)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | ₹31.9bn |
| Avg Revenue Per User | ₹201 |
| Active Users | 162M |
| Unsubscribed Users | 12.59M |

A **Before 5G / After 5G** toggle lets users switch the entire report's context between the two periods.

## Filters / Slicers

- **Month** — Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Jun (multi-select checkboxes)
- **City** — Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chandigarh, Chennai, Coimbatore, Delhi, Gurgaon, Hyderabad, and others (multi-select checkboxes)
- **Before 5G / After 5G**

## Visualizations

1. **Active Users per Month** (line chart) — active user counts from Jan–Apr (Before 5G) and Jun–Sep (After 5G), showing the shift in user base around the 5G transition.
2. **Total Plan Revenue per Month** (bar chart) — monthly plan revenue split by Before 5G and After 5G periods.
3. **Market Share Value of Company (in Crores)** (pie chart) — market share across AtliQ, Britel, DADAFONE, PIO, and Others.
4. **City-wise Market Value (in Crores)** (table) — After 5G, Before 5G, and Total market value by city (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad), with grand totals.
5. **Plan Wise Revenue in Crores** (funnel chart) — revenue generated by each plan (p1–p13), ranked from highest to lowest.
6. **AtliQ Revenue in Crores by City** (bar chart) — revenue across all cities, split by Before 5G and After 5G, ranked descending.

## Key Insights

- Active users and plan revenue both shifted notably after the 5G rollout, with the After-5G period (Jun–Sep) showing a different usage pattern than Before-5G (Jan–Apr).
- **AtliQ** holds the largest market share (35.42%) among tracked competitors, ahead of **Britel** (27.4%) and **DADAFONE** (19.5%).
- **Plan p1** is the top revenue-generating plan (₹419.93 Cr), with revenue dropping off sharply across the plan lineup down to p10 (₹13.11 Cr).
- **Mumbai** leads all cities in total market value (₹12,926.2 Cr), followed by **Delhi** and **Kolkata**.
- Revenue is fairly consistent between Before 5G and After 5G at the city level, suggesting steady performance through the transition rather than a major disruption.

## Data Fields Used (inferred)

- Month, City, 5G status (Before/After)
- Revenue, Active users, Unsubscribed users
- Plan ID, Plan revenue
- Market share by company/competitor

## Tech Stack

- **Tool:** Power BI

## How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Use the **Month** and **City** slicers on the left to filter the full report.
3. Toggle **Before 5G / After 5G** to compare performance across the two periods.
4. Hover over chart elements (line, bar, pie, funnel) for detailed tooltips.
5. KPI cards and visuals update dynamically based on selected filters.
